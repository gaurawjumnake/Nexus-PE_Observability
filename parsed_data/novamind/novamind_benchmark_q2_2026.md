# NovaMind AI — Industry Benchmark Comparison Report

**Reporting Period:** Q2 2026  |  **Prepared by:** Summit Capital Partners, Portfolio Analytics  |  **Distribution:** Investment Committee

## 1. Purpose

This report benchmarks NovaMind AI's AI ROI, governance, cost efficiency, and adoption velocity against a panel of comparable fintech portfolio companies and industry peers for Q2 2026.

## 2. AI ROI Benchmark

NovaMind's AI ROI (return on AI investment) this quarter: 3.1x. The industry median ROI (peer median AI ROI) across the benchmark panel of 14 fintech companies: 2.4x, placing NovaMind in the top quartile.

Portfolio-wide average AI ROI across all four Summit Capital AI portfolio companies (portfolio-wide AI ROI) this quarter: 2.8x.

## 4. Cost Efficiency Benchmark

Portfolio cost per outcome (portfolio unit economics AI cost) for NovaMind: $351 per successful AI-driven outcome (total AI spend of $5.2M divided by 14,820 successful outcomes delivered this quarter).
