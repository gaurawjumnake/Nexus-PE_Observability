# NovaMind AI — Sales & CRM AI Attribution Report
**Reporting Period:** Q2 2026 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Prepared by:** Revenue Operations &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Distribution:** CRO, Portfolio Operations

## 1. Win Rate Analysis

AI-assisted win rate this quarter: 52.0% (deals where the sales team used the AI deal-scoring and underwriting-assist tool). Non-AI win rate (deals closed without AI tooling): 38.0%.

This represents an AI win rate uplift of +14 percentage points this quarter, the largest gap recorded since the AI sales-assist tool was rolled out.
